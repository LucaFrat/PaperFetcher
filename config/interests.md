# Research interests

Primary focus: **robot learning** — methods that combine machine learning with
robotics to make autonomous systems more capable, sample-efficient, and robust
in the real world.

Especially interested in:

- Imitation learning, behavior cloning, diffusion policies, action chunking
- Reinforcement learning for manipulation and locomotion (sim-to-real, world models, model-based RL)
- Vision-language-action (VLA) models, multimodal foundation models for robotics
- Generalist robot policies, large-scale data collection (teleop, human video, cross-embodiment)
- Differentiable simulation, real-to-sim, sim-to-real transfer
- Tactile sensing, contact-rich manipulation, dexterous hands
- Quadrupeds and humanoids — whole-body control, learned locomotion, parkour
- Self-supervised representation learning for control

Less interested in: pure CV benchmarks without a control loop, NLP-only papers,
theory-only RL with no embodied component, autonomous driving (unless the
method is broadly applicable to other robots).
